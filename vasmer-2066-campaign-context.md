# Henrik Vasmer 2066 Campaign — Master Context Brief

**Purpose of this document:** This is the full context package for the agent building the 2066 fictional government simulator Caprica re-election campaign website for Governor Henrik Vasmer of Cambria. It contains only politically-relevant material — candidate profile, party identity, legislative record, electoral history, rivals, institutional relationships, and messaging assets — so the agent can generate on-brand copy, structure, and design without inventing lore.

## 1. THE CANDIDATE

**Henrik Vasmer** — Governor of Cambria, Chairman of the Cambrian Bloc.

- Early 40s as of 2060; would be mid-to-late 40s by 2066.
- Born and raised in **Alto**, a port town on Cambria's southern outskirts. Family were simple rural people — local fishermen and women. This origin is a core authenticity asset ("built by working people, for working people").
- Education: BA Politics, Philosophy & Economics, MA Political Psychology, PhD Rhetoric and Political Culture. (In-universe framing: these are deliberately double-edged fields — usable for genuine public good *or* manipulation. Useful as a subtle characterization note, not campaign copy.)
- Career path: Alto local council member → council spokesperson (while lecturing part-time during PhD) → Independent Governor of Cambria (2060) → founder/Chairman of the Cambrian Bloc (2062).
- Runs as an **Independent-turned-Bloc-founder**, not as a traditional party hack — "No Parties. No Handlers." was his original 2060 slogan; the Bloc's 2062 founding was framed as a "scale-up," not a pivot to machine politics.
- Cambrian Bloc is a Cambria-only regional party.
- Personally became "a bit more authoritarian" in his second term while still framed publicly as "the people's governor" — a tension the agent should be aware of but not surface explicitly in campaign copy (it's characterization, not messaging).

**Family:**
- Wife: **Major Wilhelmina "Wilma" Vasmer** (née Brandt) — Caprican military officer, Defense Research Division, classified offensive weapons systems research. Deliberately low public profile by mutual agreement due to classified work. Formally joined SDA in 2060. Multi-generational military family. **Campaign implication: she is not a visible campaign asset; do not feature her prominently or discuss her work.**
- Daughter: **Elodie Vasmer** (b. 19 Dec 2043) — BA in International Relations at Alto Regional Metropolitan University (Public). Joined SDA (not her father's Cambrian Bloc) on her own political identity, targeting the Cambria MP seat in the Nov 9, 2064 election on her own merit — **not** as Henrik's dynastic successor. **Campaign implication: father-daughter relationship can be humanized in bio/family content, but her candidacy should be framed as independent, not inherited.**

**Internal structure:**
- Henrik Vasmer is **Chairman**, standing above both factions — a unifying figure, not a factional player. This is consistent with his near-75% mandates and should be preserved in messaging: he represents the whole Bloc, not one wing.
- **The Foundationalists** (market-discipline/ordoliberal wing) — Leader: **Mateo Iranzo Belvedre**. Labor lawyer, litigated against corporate consolidation in freight/agriculture, founding legal mind of the Regional Anti-Monopoly Commission. Precise, dry, argues via contracts and precedent.

**Origin story for messaging:** The Bloc was not born from ambition but necessity — the 2060 EWPA water crisis proved a lone independent governor couldn't fight federal overreach alone. This is the founding narrative to use in any "why we exist" / "our story" page.

## 4. RECORD IN OFFICE — "PROMISES KEPT" (core policy platform for site's Record/Accomplishments section)

**First term (2060–2062):**
- **Enforcement & Referrals** — used governor's referral authority against monopolistic mergers and predatory freight contracts.
- **Statutory Appointments** — filled anti-monopoly and freight timetabling boards with actual workers/stakeholders, not political appointees.
- **Administrative Reallocation** — audited and restaffed provincial offices to clear permit backlogs and support small business.
- **CMC Pipeline Act** — established the Southern Water Corridor, a jointly-owned 1,350km pipeline (Ouluksee → Sionaq Springs → Alto → Rancho Rojos → Los Campos) between Cambria, Mezata, and Costa Blanca. ₳2.7 billion total cost, split equally three ways; a Joint Water Authority (one Commissioner per region) governs operations. Cambria retains full sovereignty over its own water infrastructure.
- **Civil Defense & Firearms Liberalization Act** — created the Office of Civil Defense; licensing reform, Firearm Safety and Proficiency Program, Business Premises Carry Permits, community armories.
- **Cambria Foundations Act** — tax relief for developers building family housing/small business premises using local labor, funded entirely from regional revenue (no federal money).

**Crisis leadership:**
- **2060 wildfire season** — firefighting response was a full success (record turnout, zero casualties, minimal damage, cross-regional coordination). The real political story was Vasmer's opposition to **EWPA**, a federal bill letting the Federal Government seize local water infrastructure "in times of crisis." Vasmer's position: the Federal Government has no jurisdiction over "infrastructure of local importance." He took a de facto coordinating role among regions, kept a direct channel to Prime Minister Adriana Flash, and secured a temporary resolution — **positioning him nationally as a defender of regional sovereignty against federal overreach.** This is probably the single best "Henrik stood up for us" story available.

**Symbolic/legacy moves:**
- Commissioned a new boulevard in Alto honoring **New Jersey Roundhead**, the 10th Prime Minister of Caprica, First Republic-era leftist leader and former New Democratic Party chairman — signals continuity with historic left/labor tradition, useful for legitimacy-building with older/traditional left voters.

## 5. 2062 RE-ELECTION PLATFORM (four planks — likely template for evolving 2066 platform)

1. **Finish the Southern Water Corridor** — complete the CMC Pipeline hub by hub so no Cambrian community faces drought alone again.
2. **Deepen Southern Cooperation** — expand the Joint Water Authority model into freight corridors and coastal trade with Mezata and Costa Blanca, full sovereignty retained.
3. **Grow What We Protected** — expanded support for independent fishing fleets, cooperative housing, rural health access, family businesses vs. consolidated capital.
4. **Finish What We Started** — frames the mandate as a decisive warning to the conservative establishment and entrenched interests fighting reforms in courts/Legislature: "Cambria is not going back."

**For 2066, the agent should likely evolve these into forward-looking planks** (e.g., corridor completion becomes "corridor delivered," cooperation deepens further, etc.) rather than repeat verbatim — happy to help draft updated planks on request. Placeholders also allowed

## 7. INSTITUTIONS CREATED UNDER VASMER (regional bodies — good for "How Cambria Works For You" explainer content)

1. **Cambria Regional Anti-Monopoly Commission** — reviews freight/rural trade mergers, seats reserved for workers/small business/unions.
2. **Cambria Freight Timetabling Board** — coordinates freight scheduling, protects rural feeder routes from deprioritization.
3. **Cambria Office of Civil Defense** — firearms licensing, safety training, community armories.
4. **Cambria Coastal and Fisheries Council** — advises on catch allocation, harbor infrastructure, protections for independent fishing families.
5. **Cambria Rural Health Access Board** — oversees mobile clinic deployment, subsidized care for remote/highland settlements.

Common thread for messaging: **every board reserves seats for the actual workers affected** — a concrete, repeatable proof point for "government by the people it governs," distinct from abstract populist rhetoric.

## 8. OPPONENTS & POLITICAL LANDSCAPE

- **Cambria Christian Democrats (CCD)** — regional-only party, color #82a6e1, main opposition. Lost 2060 (Feijóo) and 2062 (Racine) badly. 5 seats in current Legislature. Likely 2066 opponent pool.
- **National conservative bloc** — People's Party (PP) + Moderate Reform (MR), cooperate nationally. These are the parties the broader long-term campaign strategy (Vasmer + Aldric von Reichel) is aimed at weakening. Federally, Pepe Marti Rutte (PP) has been Aldric's rival in Cambria's MP seat.
- **Libertarian Party of Cambria (LPC)** — 2 crossbench seats, not firmly opposed or aligned; possible persuadable bloc on civil-liberties/firearms issues given Bloc's Homestead Current positioning.
- **Cambrian Progressive Alliance (CPA)** and **Solidarity Cambria (SC)** — both governing partners, not opposition, despite not being in a formal coalition. CPA especially is Vasmer's closest ally on rural-left issues (CPA Leader Ingrid Solvang is personally close to Vasmer, early backer of CapriConnect and the CMC Pipeline Act).
- **Federal relationship:** Prime Minister Adriana Flash — direct channel maintained during EWPA dispute; relationship can be referenced as evidence of Vasmer's national-level credibility and access, without implying full federal alignment (Cambrian Bloc is regional, SDA-aligned only informally).
